STICKMAN CLASH: 1v1 ARENA FIGHTER (MOBILE & RESPONSIVE PRO EDITION)
================================================================================

HOW TO RUN:
Open 'index.html' in your browser (Double-click or use Live Server in VS Code).
Works on all devices: Mobile Phones (iOS / Android), Tablets / iPads, and Desktops.

📱 MOBILE & RESPONSIVE FEATURES:
1. 🕹️ Dual-Thumb Touch Controls:
   - Left Thumb: Ergonomic D-Pad (◀ Left, ▶ Right, ▲ Jump, ▼ Drop/Crouch)
   - Right Thumb: Combat Cluster (👊 Punch, 🦵 Kick, 💨 Dash, 🛡️ Block, ⚡ Super)
2. ⚡ Multi-Touch Support: Hold run while jumping, sliding, and punching simultaneously!
3. 📳 Tactile Haptic Feedback: Phone vibrates on punches, kicks, blocks, and KOs!
4. 📱 2-Player Split-Screen Mode: Two players can fight on the same tablet or large phone!
   - Player 1 uses buttons on the left side of the screen.
   - Player 2 uses buttons on the right side of the screen.
5. ⛶ Fullscreen & Orientation Detection:
   - One-tap fullscreen button (⛶) hides the browser bar.
   - Automatic landscape recommendation banner on mobile portrait.
6. 📐 Responsive Canvas: Scales cleanly to 100vw/100vh with Retina display sharpness.

KEYBOARD / DESKTOP CONTROLS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTION          | PLAYER 1 (Blue)          | PLAYER 2 (Red / AI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Move Left/Right | [A] / [D]                | [Left Arrow] / [Right Arrow]
Jump (Double)   | [W] or [Space]           | [Up Arrow] (or Numpad 8/0)
Dash / Dodge    | [Q] or [E] (Invincible!) | [I] or [Numpad 7]
Drop Platform   | Hold [S] on upper stage  | Hold [Down Arrow] on upper stage
Block (Shield)  | [S] or [C] (Ground only) | [Down Arrow] or [Numpad 1]
Punch (Combo)   | [F] or [J]               | [L]
Kick (Dive Kick)| [G] or [K]               | [K] or [Numpad 5]
Super Blast     | [V] or [R] (Full Meter)  | [M] or [Numpad Enter]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
