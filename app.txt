================================================================================
STICKMAN CLASH: 1v1 ARENA FIGHTER
A Fast-Paced Competitive Multiplayer Stickman Game
================================================================================

HOW TO RUN:
Open 'index.html' in your browser (Double click or use VS Code Live Server).

FEATURES:
- 👥 2 Players on 1 Keyboard (Local Co-op / Versus)
- 🤖 Single Player vs Intelligent AI Bot (with blocking and combos)
- ⚔️ Full Combat System: Jab Punch, High Kick, Shield Block, and Super Blast
- 💥 Ring-Out & Platform Physics (Knock opponents off the floating arena into the abyss!)
- 🎵 100% Zero-Dependency Web Audio Synth (Punch, Kick, Block, Jump, Super Blast, KO Fanfare)
- 📱 Optional On-Screen Touch Controls for Mobile / Tablets
- 🏆 Best of 3 Rounds Tournament Rules

CONTROLS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTION          | PLAYER 1 (Blue)          | PLAYER 2 (Red / AI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Move Left/Right | [A] / [D]                | [Left Arrow] / [Right Arrow]
Jump (Double)   | [W]                      | [Up Arrow]
Block (Shield)  | [S] (Reduces 75% Dmg)    | [Down Arrow]
Punch (Quick)   | [F]                      | [L]
Kick (Heavy)    | [G]                      | [K]
Super Blast     | [V] (Requires 100% Bar)  | [M] (Requires 100% Bar)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
