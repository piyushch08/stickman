STICKMAN CLASH: 1v1 ARENA FIGHTER (PRO CONTROLS EDITION)
================================================================================

HOW TO RUN:
Open 'index.html' in your browser (Double-click or use Live Server in VS Code).

NEW CONTROLS & COMPETITIVE MECHANICS:
1. ⚡ Input Buffering: Inputs are buffered so combos and jumps execute without lag.
2. 🦘 Coyote Time & Jump Buffering: Smooth jumps even if pressed slightly before landing or just off an edge.
3. 💨 Dash / Dodge Roll: Press [Q] (P1) or [I] (P2) for invincibility frames and rapid repositioning!
4. 🥋 3-Hit Punch Combos: Tap punch repeatedly to chain Jab -> Cross -> Uppercut Finisher!
5. 🦵 Aerial Dive Kick: Press kick while airborne to dive down and spike your opponent!
6. ⬇️ Platform Drop-Through: Hold Down [S] or [Down Arrow] on upper platforms to drop down to the lower stage.
7. 🎮 Plug-and-Play Gamepads: Connect any USB/Bluetooth game controller (Xbox, PS4/PS5, etc.) and play instantly!

CONTROL MAPPING:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTION          | PLAYER 1 (Blue)          | PLAYER 2 (Red / AI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Move Left/Right | [A] / [D]                | [Left Arrow] / [Right Arrow]
Jump (Double)   | [W] or [Space]           | [Up Arrow] (or Numpad 8/0)
Dash / Dodge    | [Q] or [E] (Invincible!) | [I] or [Numpad 7]
Drop Platform   | Hold [S] on upper stage  | Hold [Down Arrow] on upper stage
Block (Shield)  | [S] or [C] (Ground only) | [Down Arrow] or [Numpad 1]
Punch (Combo)   | [F] or [J]               | [L]
Kick (Dive Kick)| [G] or [K]               | [K] or [Numpad 5]
Super Blast     | [V] or [R] (Full Meter)  | [M] or [Numpad Enter]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAMEPAD MAPPING:
- Left Stick / D-Pad: Move & Drop
- (A) / Cross: Jump
- (X) / Square: Punch Combo
- (B) / Circle: Kick
- (Y) / Triangle: Super Blast
- LB / RB: Dash
- LT / RT: Block
